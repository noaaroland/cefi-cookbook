Finding the Data You Want
===
The CEFI data collection is complex and contains multiple regions, grids, experiments, and time frequencies. This tutorial is going to assume you know the region of interest and might have some idea of which time frequencies, data parameters, and experiment types are appropriate for you analysis. If not, you can read up on the details of the project at the [CEFI Portal](https://psl.noaa.gov/cefi_portal/#faq) or in the model [documentation section](/content/model_doc).

In the interactive availability matrix you can begin with the region of interest folded out and other regions folded in as shown below for somebody interested in the Northwest Atlantic (nwa) region.
